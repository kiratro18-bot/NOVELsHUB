THE DEAD MAN’S ALIBI

Chapter 6 — The Locker Under Platform Nine

The next morning, Shun brought bad news.

“The police have issued a warrant.”

“For what?”

“Searching the apartment where Natsumi was staying.”

Mio stood.

“How did they find it?”

Shun placed a printed document on the table.

“Someone told them.”

Toma looked at him.

“Who?”

“That’s the problem. The request came from Detective Nakamura.”

Silence.

Toma picked up his phone.

He called the detective.

No answer.

Again.

Nothing.

Then a message arrived.

Meet me. No friends. No police station. 5 PM.

Toma showed Shun.

“Absolutely not,” Shun said.

“Didn’t ask.”

“You’re going to get kidnapped.”

“Then you can say I told you so.”

Shun looked at Mio.

“Can you talk some sense into him?”

Mio shrugged.

“I’ve tried.”

“Wonderful.”

5:00 PM — Riverside Park

Nakamura was sitting on a bench.

He didn’t look surprised when Toma arrived.

“You’re difficult to contact.”

“You’ve been avoiding me.”

“Correct.”

“Why?”

Nakamura gestured towards the bench.

“Sit.”

Toma remained standing.

“Talk.”

The detective sighed.

“You found Ono.”

Toma didn’t answer.

“You found the locker.”

Still nothing.

Nakamura looked tired.

“Then you’ve probably heard that Kaito never died in an accident.”

Toma’s jaw tightened.

“Why did you tell everyone he did?”

“I didn’t.”

“What?”

“I was a junior officer when the incident happened. I received an order to identify the body. I filed a preliminary report. The final identification was completed by another department.”

“Who?”

“Chief Inspector Ueda.”

Toma remembered the name.

“He’s involved?”

Nakamura nodded.

“I began questioning the report a year later. My evidence disappeared. My supervisor told me to stop.”

“And you did?”

The detective looked away.

“Yes.”

Toma laughed bitterly.

“Excellent detective work.”

“I know.”

Nakamura accepted the insult.

“I’ve been trying to reopen the investigation quietly. But someone has been moving the evidence.”

“Why?”

“Because Northbridge is being demolished. Whatever they hid in that terminal will disappear with it.”

Toma sat down.

“What happened to my father?”

Nakamura took a small envelope from his coat.

“I don’t know.”

“That’s it?”

“I don’t know whether he’s alive, dead, hiding, or working with them.”

Toma stared at him.

“But I know one thing.”

Nakamura leaned forward.

“Your father wasn’t the only person who disappeared that night.”

He handed over a document.

MASAKI KURODA.

“Your missing girl’s father?”

“Yes.”

The report contained an old photograph.

Two men stood outside a construction office.

Kaito Kisaragi.

Masaki Kuroda.

Between them was a third person.

A young woman.

Natsumi stared at the photograph when Toma showed it to her.

She recognised her father immediately.

But the woman...

She looked closer.

“My mother.”

Toma glanced at her.

“Your mother?”

Natsumi nodded.

“She’s supposed to be dead.”

The group went silent.

Nakamura’s expression changed.

“That’s impossible.”

Natsumi turned the photograph over.

A date.

Six years earlier.

And a location.

NORTHBRIDGE TERMINAL — EASTERN ENTRANCE.

She looked at Toma.

“My mother didn’t die.”
