THE DEAD MAN’S ALIBI

Chapter 9 — The Dead Man’s Alibi

November 3 — 8:15 AM

The group gathered at Shun’s apartment.

There were now six people.

Toma.

Mio.

Shun.

Natsumi.

Yuri.

And Kaito.

Nobody was particularly comfortable with the arrangement.

Shun was the first to speak.

“So...”

He looked at Kaito.

“You’re actually alive.”

Kaito nodded.

Shun stared at him.

Then turned towards Toma.

“Your family is exhausting.”

Toma threw a cushion at him.

They examined the original documents Kaito had brought.

Four suspects.

Four separate cases.

Four different alibis.

And one dead man.

The records revealed the truth.

Genda had instructed his associates to use Kaito’s identity because the company knew that any claim of meeting him would create confusion.

A dead man couldn’t confirm or deny an alibi.

The police would waste time investigating sightings that could never be properly verified.

Meanwhile, Northbridge continued moving evidence.

The stolen records.

The assault on Ono.

Masaki’s disappearance.

The attempted depot fire.

All parts of the same operation.

Mio looked at the ledger.

“There’s another name.”

Toma leaned over.

REIJI NAKAMURA.

Shun frowned.

“Why is the detective listed?”

Kaito examined the entry.

“Payment for evidence transfer.”

Silence.

Shun stood.

“Then Nakamura is involved.”

“Not necessarily,” Mio replied.

“His name is here.”

“Look at the date.”

She pointed.

The payment was dated six years earlier.

The same day Kaito disappeared.

Toma looked at the entry again.

“Maybe he was part of the original investigation.”

Kaito nodded.

“He was a junior officer. I remember him.”

“Did he take money?”

“I don’t know.”

Shun grabbed his jacket.

“I’m going to find out.”

10:42 AM — Police Headquarters

Nakamura was waiting.

He looked at the ledger.

Then at Kaito.

For a moment, the detective seemed unable to speak.

“You...”

Kaito nodded.

“Hello, Reiji.”

Nakamura sat down.

He took off his glasses and rubbed his eyes.

“Six years.”

“I know.”

“Do you people have any idea how much paperwork I’ve done because of you?”

Kaito almost smiled.

Toma didn’t.

Nakamura examined the payment entry.

Then shook his head.

“I received no money.”

“Why is your name here?” Shun asked.

“Because I filed the preliminary report. Ueda used my credentials to move the evidence.”

He opened a drawer and removed a second folder.

“I kept copies.”

Inside were original case logs and a report showing that the final identification of Kaito’s body had been changed after the initial investigation.

Nakamura looked at Toma.

“I should have spoken up.”

“Yes.”

“I was afraid.”

Toma nodded.

“Everyone seems to have been afraid.”

Nakamura accepted the remark.

Then he stood.

“You’re going to need more than these documents.”

Mio looked at him.

“What do you mean?”

“Genda has already scheduled the demolition. The terminal will be cleared by noon.”

He checked the clock.

“We have less than ninety minutes.”

11:16 AM — Northbridge Terminal

The old terminal was surrounded by construction fencing.

Workers were preparing the demolition site.

A security vehicle stood near the entrance.

Masato Genda was there.

He looked nothing like a criminal.

Expensive suit.

Polished shoes.

A calm smile.

He was speaking to a group of officials when Toma approached.

Genda noticed him.

Then smiled.

“Mr Kisaragi.”

Toma stopped.

“Do I know you?”

Genda laughed.

“Your father and I were colleagues.”

“Funny. He never mentioned you.”

The smile weakened.

Kaito stepped forward.

“Hello, Genda.”

For a moment, the director’s face changed.

Then he recovered.

“You’re supposed to be dead.”

“People keep telling me that.”

Genda glanced towards the police vehicles entering the site.

Then towards Ueda, who stood near the demolition equipment.

He understood.

The game was over.

But he still smiled.

“You have no idea what you’re doing.”

Toma looked at him.

“Actually, I think we do.”

Genda leaned closer.

“Your father could have kept his family safe. He chose to play hero.”

Kaito said nothing.

Genda continued.

“Look where that got him.”

Toma stepped forward.

“Enough.”

The director looked at him.

“You think you’ve won?”

Toma held up the ledger.

“No.”

He paused.

“But you haven’t buried it this time.”

Nakamura approached.

“Masato Genda, you’re being detained pending investigation into fraud, evidence tampering, attempted arson and the disappearance of Masaki Kuroda.”

Genda’s smile finally disappeared.

He looked towards Ueda.

The inspector turned away.

Genda was escorted towards the police vehicle.

He didn’t resist.

He simply looked at Toma one last time.

“Your father is still a dead man, boy.”

Toma watched him leave.

“Not anymore.”
