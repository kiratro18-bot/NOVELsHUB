The Petal That Falls With A Smile — Volume III

The Tree

POV: Ren

There she was.

Sora.

Sitting beneath the sakura tree.

It was blooming — soft pink petals drifting slowly, like the world had decided to slow down just for her. I stopped a few steps away, suddenly unsure if I should interrupt.

Calm down, Ren. Just talk.

I walked closer.

“Sora.”

She looked up and smiled. “Oh… hi, Ren.”

She didn’t move. Just patted the space beside her.

I sat down.

For a moment, neither of us spoke. The petals kept falling.

Then my heart did something stupid — it sped up.

“Sora,” I said quickly, before I could lose the courage, “are you free this Sunday?”

She blinked. “Sunday?”

“Y-yeah,” I scratched the back of my neck. “If you are, I mean.”

She tilted her head slightly. “Why? Do you need help with something?”

“No— I mean—” I inhaled. “There’s this movie. I thought maybe… you’d like to watch it with me.”

A movie.

I waited.

Her eyes softened.

Thoughts — Sora:

With him… a movie? I’d love that.

“Yes,” she said. “I will.”

It took me a second to process.

“R-really?”

She nodded. “Yeah.”

I tried to stay calm, but inside—

I said it. I actually said it.

“Then,” I smiled, “it’s a date.”

She didn’t deny it.

The petals kept falling.

POV: Haruki

Night.

My room was quiet.

Too quiet.

I lay on my bed, staring at the ceiling, replaying the last few days over and over.

Noa’s smile.

Her laugh.

The way she walked beside me without asking for anything.

And suddenly… it was clear.

I sat up.

“So that’s it,” I muttered.

I wasn’t confused anymore.

Not about Sora.

Not about the past.

Not about myself.

“I’ll confess,” I said aloud.

Not loudly. Not dramatically.

“I’ll tell her how I feel.”

I already knew where.

Under that damn sakura tree.

The place where things once ended… would become the place where something new begins.

I looked out the window.

Spring had come again.

And this time — I wasn’t afraid of it.

Narrator

Some trees witness heartbreak.

Some witness healing.

And some…

Wait patiently for the right love to arrive.
