The Petal That Falls With A Smile — Volume IV

She Understood

POV: Sora

Ren and I sat together, eating quietly.

The festival noise surrounded us — laughter, music, footsteps — but none of it reached me.

That’s when I saw them.

Haruki.

Noa.

Walking together through the crowd.

Just the two of them.

…A date?

The thought slipped in before I could stop it.

Something inside me cracked.

Not loudly.

Not all at once.

Just enough to hurt.

Was that love? I wondered.

Or was it just the weight of something I never chose properly?

Haruki had said he moved on.

And now I could see it.

He really had.

Then why did it still hurt me?

I looked at Noa — the way she stayed close to him, the way she smiled.

She loves him.

And I knew something else too.

I hurt him first.

I ran.

I stayed silent.

I chose fear.

This… this was the consequence.

For the first time, I understood what it meant to be on the other side of regret.

“I have to move on,” I whispered to myself.

Not because I wanted to.

Because I had to.

POV: Ren

She went quiet.

“Sora… Sora?”

She blinked and looked at me. “Oh. Yeah?”

“Let’s go try that game over there,” I said, pointing.

“Yeah,” she nodded. “Let’s go.”

She stood up, smiling softly.

But I could tell — her mind was still somewhere else.

I think I’m falling for her again, I thought.

Slowly. Carefully.

But the fear followed right after.

Does she feel the same… or am I just convenient right now?

POV: Haruki

We walked side by side.

The lights reflected in Noa’s eyes.

She looked… beautiful.

And it startled me.

Why am I noticing this now?

I’ve always known this… haven’t I?

The thought slipped out before I could stop it.

“Noa,” I said, “do you have a crush on someone?”

Idiot, I scolded myself immediately.

She froze.

Then—

She blushed.

“Y-yes… I do.”

My heart skipped — not painfully, not sharply.

Just… honestly.

She looked nervous now, fingers tightening around her sleeve.

And for the first time, I realized something clearly:

She wasn’t waiting for my past to disappear.

She was standing here — now.

Narrator

That night, under festival lights—

One girl learned what it meant to let go.

One boy wondered if love had been beside him all along.

And another hoped that friendship could become something more.

Not every understanding comes with relief.

Some come quietly.

And stay.

End of Chapter
