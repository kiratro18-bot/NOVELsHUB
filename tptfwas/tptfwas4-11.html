The Petal That Falls With A Smile — Volume IV

Wedding

Scene: The Groom's Room

Haruki is getting ready for his wedding when Ren walks in.

Ren: Haruki! Haruki! You're getting married too soon, brooo! You're leaving me all alone in this generation!

Note: They're bros now.

Haruki: Yeah. I made my decision when I asked her out.

Ren: Damn! Being the single guy is tough...

Haruki: What about you?

Ren: Me? I'll get married soon, too!

A bell rings.

Ren: Let's go. The time has come...

Scene: The Bride's Room

Sora: All set, Nao! My girl, you look so beautiful in that wedding dress! I should marry you instead!

Nao: Hehe. I'm nervous and happy about this day...

Sora: I know you are, and...

A bell rings.

Sora: And... Oh! Let's go, shall we?

Nao: Yes...

Scene: The Wedding Hall

The groom stands at the podium, waiting for his bride to arrive.

Then, the bride appears.

She walks slowly toward him, each step echoing through the hall.

Finally, she stands in front of him.

The groom gently lifts the veil covering her face.

For a moment, Haruki can only stare.

Nao looks so beautiful. There's something different about seeing her dressed as a bride, and Haruki can't help feeling shy.

The ceremony continues.

After the officiant completes the ceremony, he smiles at the newlyweds.

Officiant: Now, you may kiss the bride.

Haruki leans toward Nao, and they share their first kiss as a married couple.

And so, the wedding celebrations begin...

Haruki and Nao

Haruki: Thank you, Nao, for marrying me...

Nao: Dummy! I should be the one thanking you...

They both laugh.

Then, Haruki surprises everyone by preparing a speech.

Haruki: Hello, everyone! Thank you for coming to our wedding.

I'd like to say a few words about what I'm feeling today...

Nao, the day we met after exchanging letters was the day I gained all of this.

Everything I've experienced with you is something I'll cherish forever.

Every little moment, every memory we've made together...

Thank you for everything, Nao.

And thank you for being by my side.

I'll cherish it all, forever.

Thank you.

Haruki finishes his speech.

Nao looks at him with a smile.

Nao: Now, now... It's my turn!

Haruki smiles and listens.

Nao: Haruki, do you remember the university fireworks?

Yeah... That time with you was my true happiness.

Or, I should say, one of my happiest memories.

Having you by my side is the greatest happiness I could ever ask for.

Thank you, Haruki...

She smiles at him.

Nao: Thank you for being with me.

Haruki smiles back at her.

Narrator

The petal that blooms in an odd season... Isn't it interesting?

Somehow, this story is different.

These people bloomed in their own time.

Through all the seasons, all the misunderstandings, and all the moments they thought they might lose each other, they found their way forward.

And now, they have bloomed and become bride and groom.

Perhaps their story is stronger than even I am.

Epilogue

After Two Years

Scene: Haruki and Nao's Home

Haruki: Nao! Are you and Minato ready yet? We'll be late for the wedding!

Minato is their baby boy, one year and two months old.

Nao: Yes! Let's go, honey!

She finishes getting ready, and the family heads out together.

Scene: The Wedding Hall

Ren stands at the podium, waiting for his bride.

From a distance, Haruki watches him with a mischievous smile.

Haruki: Hey, Ren! You said you'd get married soon, but I didn't think you'd actually do it!

Ren: Shut up, bro!

Haruki laughs.

Then, the bride appears.

Sora slowly walks toward the podium, her footsteps echoing through the hall.

Ren watches her approach.

The girl standing before him is the childhood friend he has known for so long.

The girl he once thought he had lost.

The girl he finally found his way back to.

The ceremony continues, and the officiant completes the wedding rites.

Officiant: You may now kiss the bride.

Ren gently kisses Sora.

And so, the wedding celebrations begin for the newlyweds.

POV: The Sakura Tree

I've seen so many faces sitting beneath my branches.

Some came here to grieve.

Some came here to laugh.

Some came here to find peace.

And some came here without knowing that their lives were about to change.

I've witnessed more stories than any ordinary human could imagine.

I bloom in an odd season.

And I've watched odd people grow, fall in love, and create their own stories.

Somehow, their stories have become stronger than I am.

They've endured the changing seasons.

They've endured the passing of time.

They've endured the moments when happiness seemed so far away.

And yet, they continued to bloom.

Perhaps that's what makes their stories so beautiful.

Even a petal that falls can leave behind a story worth remembering.

And even after falling, it can still fall with a smile.
