The Petal That Falls With A Smile — Volume IV

Lie

Scene: Outside the Movie Theater
Ren's POV

I'm late!

I told her the show was in the afternoon, and I'm already five minutes late.

As Ren arrives, he sees Sora standing near the pink tree, waiting for him.

Ren's thoughts:

She looks so beautiful...

Ren: Ah, what am I saying?!

He approaches her.

Ren: Hel-hello, Sora.

Sora: Hello, Ren.

Ren: Shall we?

Sora: Yes, let's go...

Awkwardness fills the air.

Sora: What movie are we watching?

Ren: Ah, the title is The Petal That Blooms in Odd Season.

Sora: Oh, sounds interesting.

Ren: It is interesting! I watched the promo.

Sora: So, do you know any spoilers?

Ren: No!

Both of them laugh a little.

They reach the movie theater and watch the film.

During an emotional scene, tears well up in Sora's eyes as the boy in the movie finally confesses his feelings to the girl after all the drama.

Ren hands Sora a tissue.

Sora: Thank you...

After the Movie

Sora: It was beautiful. I wonder who wrote it...

Ren: Yeah, I know who wrote it.

Sora: Who?

Ren: An underrated writer whose pen name is AKA.

Sora: AKA? As in red?

Ren: We don't know.

Sora: Huh... So mysterious.

Ren: Yeah. Anyway, shall we go to our next spot?

Sora: Where?

Ren: A café.

Sora: Sure!

As they walk toward the café, an awkward silence settles between them again.

Then Ren finally breaks it.

Ren: Sora, you can ignore this question if you want...

Why did you lie to me back then?

Sora: BECAUSE I HAD TO!

She pauses.

Sora: Sorry...

Ren: I'm sorry for asking...

Sora: No, wait. Let me clear this up...

I was getting transferred back then, and if I'd given you an answer, I would've hurt you...

The thought of hurting you scares me.

Ren: Oh...

End of Chapter
