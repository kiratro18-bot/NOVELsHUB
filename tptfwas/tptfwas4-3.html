The Petal That Falls With A Smile — Volume III

A Date

POV: Noa

The festival was over.

The noise, the lights, the crowd — all gone.

Our class was supposed to celebrate together after everything, but one by one the messages came in.

Can’t make it.

Too tired.

Something came up.

And just like that… the party was cancelled.

Haruki and I were the first to arrive.

And the last.

For a moment, we just stood there, awkwardly.

“So…” I said, trying to sound casual. “What do we do now?”

My cheeks felt warm. I hated how obvious I was.

Haruki looked at his phone, then at me, and smiled — not forced, not confused.

Calm.

Happy.

“I don’t know,” he said. “But let’s still celebrate.”

I blinked. “Celebrate… us?”

“Well,” he shrugged lightly, “we survived the festival, didn’t we?”

I laughed. “I guess we did.”

“What do you feel like eating?” he asked.

That simple question made my heart jump.

Not what should we eat.

Not do you want to go home.

What I wanted.

“Anything is fine,” I said quickly. “I mean— maybe something warm?”

“Alright,” he nodded. “Then let’s do that.”

And just like that, we started walking.

Side by side.

POV: Haruki

Wow.

I didn’t expect this.

Alone with Noa… and it didn’t feel awkward.

No pressure.

No past chasing me.

No confusion.

Just quiet comfort.

Somewhere between walking and talking, it hit me.

I’m… developing feelings for her.

Not because she was always there.

Not because she waited.

But because right now — in this moment — I wanted to be here.

With her.

The realization surprised me.

Love wasn’t loud.

It didn’t crash into me.

It arrived gently.

I stopped walking.

“Noa,” I said.

She turned to me immediately. “Y-yes?”

My heart was racing now.

“This,” I said, gesturing around us, “this feels like a date.”

Her eyes widened.

Her face turned red.

Honestly?

Mine too.

“A-a date?” she repeated softly.

“Yeah,” I said, letting out a small breath. “If you’re okay with that.”

For a second, she didn’t answer.

Then she smiled — not shy, not scared.

Just happy.

“I am,” she said. “I really am.”

“Then,” I smiled back, “let’s order something.”

She nodded quickly. “Y-yeah.”

And for the first time in a long while, I felt like I wasn’t looking backward anymore.

Narrator

Some love stories begin with fireworks.

Others begin after they end.

No crowds.

No confessions shouted into the night.

Just two people choosing to stay.

Together.

End of Chapter
