After
the Rain

Romance Epilogue By AKA
Three years later. ☕

A small café on the corner of a quiet street, tucked between a used bookstore and a flower shop that always smelled like lavender.

The afternoon sun poured in through the windows, and the bell above the door chimed softly as Hiro stepped inside.

He carried a folded umbrella in one hand and a paper bag in the other — inside, a sandwich (too much mayo, still), and a rice ball wrapped in cling film.

At a corner table, she was already waiting.

Sketchbook open. Pencil in hand.

Mei looked up when she saw him, her eyes crinkling just slightly. Her hair was longer now, and she wore glasses when she drew, something she once said she hated.

He loved it.

“You’re late,” she said.

“You’re early.”

They exchanged smiles. He sat down, placing the paper bag in front of her.

“Still putting apples in tuna?” she teased.

“Tradition.”

She rolled her eyes, but unwrapped the rice ball anyway.

Outside, rain tapped gently against the windows. Just a drizzle — soft enough to make the world feel slower.

Mei turned her sketchbook toward him.

✏️ Mei's drawing

On the page, two people sat at a table just like this, one holding a sandwich, the other smiling.
She had drawn this exact moment. Before it even happened.

“I’ve been working on something,” she said. “A short comic. Ten scenes. Quiet ones. About a boy who always showed up.”

Hiro’s heart pulled at the corners.

“What’s it called?”

She looked at him, then said — quietly, like the words still meant more than they could hold:

“Ten Percent of Forever.”

He didn’t answer.

Just reached across the table and held her hand.

The rain outside whispered.

And this time, neither of them needed an umbrella.
